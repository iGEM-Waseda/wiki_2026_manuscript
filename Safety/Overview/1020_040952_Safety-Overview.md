In Project Safety, we needed to evaluate the safety of each module comprehensively, from laboratory experiments through to real-world implementation. To this end, we constructed our own Progressive Safety Assessment Framework (PSAF), tailored to SUNRISE, and on this basis we evaluated the safety of both the Cargo Protein Injection (CPI) and Targeted Protein Degradation (TPD) modules. 

**In PSAF**, we first divided the project life cycle into four stages: Contained Research Safety / Unintended Exposure Safety / Intended-Use Safety / Life-cycle & Deployment Safety. Next, for each stage, we identified risks from three perspectives — Biological Components / Engineered Function / Process・Use — and we examined each identified risk according to the following procedure: Hazardous Property → Exposure Scenario → Consequence → Control → Evidence & Uncertainty. Assessing the project along this framework allowed us to grasp the risks comprehensively and to respond to and evaluate them appropriately.→ For details, see PSAF (link to PSAF) 

**In CPI safety**, we evaluated, in accordance with PSAF, the safety of CPI, the module responsible for delivering proteins into target bacteria. Examining the risks at each of the four stages — the experimental stage, environmental release, therapeutic use, and real-world implementation — gave us a comprehensive evaluation of the risks associated with CPI. →For details, see CPI Module Safety  (link to CPI) 

**In TPD safety**, we evaluated, in accordance with PSAF, the safety of TPD, the module that binds to a target protein, directs it to the bacterium’s endogenous protein degradation machinery, and thereby causes it to be degraded. We examined the risks along the same four stages as for CPI, covering the risks related to TPD comprehensively.→ For details, see TPD Module Safety (link to TPD)
